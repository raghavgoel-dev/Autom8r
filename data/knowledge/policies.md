# Autom8r Demo Communications — Policies

> Demo content for a fictional company. Not a real business.

## Refund Policy

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all subscription plans, counted
from the date of the first payment. If the platform does not fit your needs,
write to support within 14 days for a full refund. Setup fees for custom
integrations are non-refundable once work has started.

## Service Level and Uptime

We target 99.5 percent monthly uptime for the messaging platform. Scheduled
maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance and never during
business hours (9:00 to 21:00 IST).

## Support Hours and Escalation

Standard support: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 21:00 IST, via WhatsApp and
email, with a first response within 4 business hours. Growth and Enterprise
customers get a named account manager and priority escalation.

## Data Retention

Conversation transcripts and lead records are retained for 12 months on
active plans and deleted within 30 days of account closure. Customers may
request earlier deletion of their end-customer data at any time.

## Fair Use

Automated conversations must comply with WhatsApp Business and TRAI
regulations: no unsolicited promotional messages, and every recipient must
have opted in. Accounts that send spam are suspended.

## Privacy

We act as a data processor for our customers. We never sell end-customer
data, never use conversation content to train models without written
consent, and sign a data processing agreement on request.
