# Autom8r Demo Communications — FAQ

> Demo content for a fictional company. Not a real business.

## What does Autom8r Demo Communications do?

We help small and mid-size businesses automate customer conversations. Our
platform answers routine customer enquiries on WhatsApp, SMS, and voice,
qualifies incoming leads, and hands complex conversations to human agents.

## Which channels do you support?

We support WhatsApp Business API, SMS, and voice calls. WhatsApp is the most
popular channel for support automation. Email integration is on the roadmap
but not available today.

## How much does it cost?

Starter plan: ₹4,999 per month for up to 1,000 automated conversations.
Growth plan: ₹12,999 per month for up to 10,000 conversations, plus lead
scoring. Enterprise plans are custom-priced above that. All prices are demo
figures for this fictional company.

## How long does setup take?

A typical WhatsApp support automation goes live in 5 to 7 working days,
including template approval and a short pilot. Voice bot setups take about
two weeks.

## Do you support Indian languages?

Yes. The assistant can converse in English, Hindi, and Hinglish out of the
box, and we can enable additional Indian languages on the Growth plan.

## Can a human agent take over a conversation?

Yes. Any conversation can be escalated to a human agent. The handoff
includes the full chat transcript and the collected lead details so the
customer never has to repeat themselves.

## Is my customer data safe?

All demo data is stored locally in this project. In a real deployment, data
would be encrypted in transit and at rest, and customers could request
deletion at any time. See the policies document for retention details.

## Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, a 14-day pilot with up to 200 automated conversations, no credit card
required. A pilot is the easiest way to measure containment rate before
committing.
