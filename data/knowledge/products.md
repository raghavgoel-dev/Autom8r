# Autom8r Demo Communications — Products

> Demo content for a fictional company. Not a real business.

## WhatsApp Customer Support Automation

Our flagship product. An AI assistant answers routine WhatsApp enquiries —
order status, appointment booking, pricing questions, business hours — and
escalates anything unusual to your team. Typical customers automate 60 to 80
percent of incoming queries. Best for businesses receiving more than 300
WhatsApp enquiries per month.

## SMS Campaigns and Alerts

Send transactional SMS (OTP, confirmations, reminders) and promotional
campaigns with delivery reports. Includes template management and DLT
registration guidance for India.

## Voice Bots

AI voice agents that answer inbound calls, capture intent, and route to the
right team. Supports English and Hindi. Useful for businesses that still
receive most enquiries by mid-day phone calls.

## Lead Qualification Agent

The very technology this demo runs on. The agent chats with a prospect,
collects name, phone, business type, requirement, and monthly enquiry
volume, then scores and prioritizes the lead so sales teams call the hottest
leads first.

## Analytics Dashboard

Weekly reports on conversation volume, containment rate (chats resolved
without a human), response times, and lead pipeline. Included on Growth and
Enterprise plans.

## Integrations

Leads can sync to Google Sheets out of the box (as this project
demonstrates), and the tool layer is built on MCP so new systems — a CRM, a
ticketing tool, a calendar — can be added as MCP tools without changing the
assistant.
